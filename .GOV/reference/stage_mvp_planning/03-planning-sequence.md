# Stage planning sequence

## Phase 1: Full-feature planning and research in this reference workspace

1. Complete source-stub lineage, reaffirmed-or-superseded disposition, and adjacency mapping without inheriting old Stage implementation.
2. Research current Servo, Chromium-bootstrap, privacy, security, agent-control, compatibility, and embedding implementation patterns.
3. Define the Stage kernel, Servo-primary architecture, minimal Chromium boundary, and transition rules.
4. Define sessions, permissions, Stage Apps, Bridge, capture/import, artifact portability, ASR lineage, diagnostics, and validation behavior.
5. Red-team plausible security, compatibility, data-loss, state-bleed, bootstrap-permanence, and maintenance failures.
6. Define complete browser-product, authentication, model-control, accessibility, localization, manual, and operator-support workflows.
7. Define persistence/events/concurrency, current active-WP contracts, legacy Stage removal/optional real-data import, backup/recovery, packaging/update/supply-chain, safe mode, incident, validation, and evidence contracts.
8. Produce stable requirement/gate/readiness registries and an expanded future 100+ microtask lane/DAG blueprint without generating official MT files.
9. Assemble proposed master-spec Stage-topic enrichments without editing the spec.

## Phase 2: Operator architecture checkpoint

The operator reviews the complete initial plan and locks, rejects, or corrects the architecture, scope, renderer transition, acceptance gates, and major open decisions.

## Phase 3: Master-spec Stage-topic rewrite

After the operator concludes initial planning, rewrite the master-spec Stage topic from the approved plan, update its architecture and privacy posture, and validate the spec bundle through its canonical workflow.

## Phase 4: Work-packet finalization

Author the replacement Stage MVP contract from the current corpus, record old-stub supersession and lineage without inheriting old Stage authority, finalize refinement and red-team detail, and pass the required approval/signature gates.

## Phase 5: Packet and microtask creation

Create the official packet and 100+ independently claimable microtasks with stable lane IDs, dependencies, file ownership, acceptance criteria, verification commands, status synchronization, and whole-WP integration gates.
