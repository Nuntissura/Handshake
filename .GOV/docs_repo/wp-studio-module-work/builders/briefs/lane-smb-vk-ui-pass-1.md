## Pixel tolerance and why

- Exact, difference 0: Rect, Edge, Checker, opaque Image, scissor edges, and every pixel the painter wrote at full coverage with an opaque colour. Integer quads put every pixel centre strictly inside or outside, and an opaque source converts back exactly: 'If r is equal to an integer, then that integer value must be returned' (Vulkan fundamentals.adoc, Conversion From Floating-Point to Normalized Fixed-Point). So AC-GEOM-03 shared edges and AC-COL-01 tokens hold bit for bit, as B2 done_when asks (flat colours equal tokens exactly).
- At most 1 per channel per blend: glyphs, icons, translucent Image and Ramp. Coverage is the same integer through the atlas, but blending is 'treated as if carried out in floating-point' at a precision no lower than the destination (framebuffer.adoc), and the float-to-UNORM step returns one of the two closest values and only 'should round to nearest' (fundamentals.adoc), while the painter rounds the exact rational (paint.rs:62-63).
